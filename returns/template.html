04 — For the product team

Quality watchlist

SKUs ranked by returns carrying a product-fault signal: disappointed in quality, finish not as pictured, damaged or defective, incorrect item sent, or a free-text follow-up of Size or Function. The second figure in the flagged column is the number of distinct orders behind those units: twelve complaints from one customer is one data point, twelve from twelve customers is a pattern. The confidence dot reads distinct orders, not units.

The trend column plots flagged units for each month held in this file, and always spans the full period even when you have filtered to one month. The last point is hollow and dashed because it is structurally incomplete — returns carry the date of the order, and returns against March orders were still landing in May — so the direction arrow reads only across the settled months and ignores it. Hover a sparkline for the monthly counts.

06 — Investigation list

Category tracker

click a category to open its top 10 SKUs

Categories sorted by value returned. Return rate is returned units over units sold in the same window, so a SKU that sold in December and came back in February will read high. Rates on fewer than 20 units sold are greyed — they move too much to act on. On the SKU rows, returned units are shown over the number of distinct orders behind them, with ◆ marking a count where a single order holds half or more — 82 units from one customer is not the same finding as 82 from twenty. Category rows show plain units: at that level concentration is mild enough that the extra figure would imply precision the aggregate does not have.
