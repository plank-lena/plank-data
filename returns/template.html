    05 — Investigation list

    Category tracker

    click to drill: category → subcategory → SKU
    Sort category/subcategory rows by

    The single investigation surface (the old separate quality watchlist is folded in here). Category and subcategory rows sort by value returned by default — toggle above to sort by return rate instead; any column header can also be clicked directly. SKUs within a subcategory always rank by a combined rank of % orders returned and return value together (ruling 3) — a high rate on a cheap, low-volume SKU is a small problem, a high value on a low rate is a volume effect, and neither alone tells you where to look first. SKUs need at least 20 orders sold in the period to appear — below that a rate moves too much on a handful of orders to act on. Family is shown on each SKU row (e.g. KEPLER 160/220/280 read as one product in three sizes) — v1 doesn't add a separate family axis, just the label.

    On SKU rows, ◆ next to the return value marks a SKU where a single order holds half or more of its returned units — a concentrated single-customer event, not a pattern. Reason is the dominant stated reason for that SKU; review is the matching Yotpo signal — exact SKU where flagged, else the family's review themes, else a dash (the review corpus is separate and sparse — see §07's warning).
